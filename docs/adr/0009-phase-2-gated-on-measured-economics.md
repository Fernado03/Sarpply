# Phase 2 spend is gated on measured Route economics, not on the calendar

No Phase 2 engineering starts until the Phase 1 spreadsheet records, for two consecutive weeks, a Route clearing RM 1,500 GMV and RM 150 gross profit, with per-Stall per-SKU quantities captured from real orders and onboarding self-reports. The 16-week roadmap's Week 5 start was rejected as a trigger: it begins automating a model whose central inputs — basket size, the drink-stall anchor's creamer volume, and Cluster conversion — were assumptions, and a spreadsheet costing nothing per week is a far cheaper place to discover that a Route does not clear than a schema is.

## Consequences

If the gate is not cleared, Phase 2 does not start, whatever the calendar says. Measured baskets replace the three invented basket tiers in every downstream projection, and if `D1` collapses because the drink operator already holds a factory account, the Route still clears the gate without it — RM 2,047 GMV and RM 209 gross profit — so the gate survives its most load-bearing assumption failing.
